## Platform

## Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, and peers evaluating Mukul Singh's software engineering capabilities and past projects.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio to showcase full-stack development skills, display work experience (Saarthi AI, Cloud Maven), feature live SaaS projects (Base0, SupaToken.lol), and provide a central hub for social links (GitHub, LinkedIn). Success means earning technical credibility and professional connections.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Must integrate live GitHub contribution data.
- Must integrate a live Spotify "Currently Playing" widget.
- Next.js (App Router), Tailwind CSS, TypeScript, and `@deemlol/next-icons`.

## Evidence on Hand
- Resume data (Mukul Singh, Jaipur India, Full-stack developer).
- Project details (Base0 SaaS, SupaToken.lol).

## Product Principles
- **Engineering first**: The interface should reflect technical competence through structure and precision.
- **Content over chrome**: Let the work (projects, commits) be the focal point.
